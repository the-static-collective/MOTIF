## Why this is called Quantum Code

Across the user's June–November 2026 conversations, **QUANTUM CODE / IT WRITES ITSELF** repeatedly meant *a relational generative grammar whose outputs can become inputs to subsequent transformations*, with continuity and revision preserved in external artifacts. This is a project-local name. It does **not** claim a quantum computer, consciousness-triggered collapse, or a discovered physical base code.

The National Treasure flagship case describes **FIELD → LOCAL CONSTRAINT → CONFIGURATION → REALIZED HISTORY → RECURSION** and separates historical, linguistic, mathematical, and fundamental-physics claims. Its [claim QC-030](https://github.com/the-static-collective/national-treasure/blob/main/cases/quantum-code-glyph-circuit/CLAIMS.md) calls the architecture a *working definition*, not physics evidence.

This first trial deliberately uses the smallest tractable portion of that program.

## Predeclared question

Can a **decoder-sensitive, independently locally reviewed finite transition table** enable *recursive glyph-world traversal* that a frozen two-step grammar cannot, while preserving distinct formation histories and refusing an unvalidated attractive shortcut?

If yes, that demonstrates the benefit of conditioning and validated grammar expansion in this **designed toy environment**; it is **not** independent algorithm invention.

## Formal object

A transition is a tuple:

    (DECODER, CURRENT_TOPOLOGY, OPERATION) -> NEXT_TOPOLOGY

The finite topology inventory is:

    POINT, AXIS, FORK, CUP, LOOP

These are abstract labels, **not** Hebrew characters, claims of letter evolution, quantum states, or universal geometric invariants.

- **Carrier:** current topology.
- **Decoder / receiving world:** a named context A or B. It affects legal outcomes.
- **Operation:** an action vocabulary such as extend, branch, curve, close.
- **Formation trace:** immutable sequence of locally previewed steps with rule-source checksums.
- **Re-entry:** each output topology becomes the next operation's input.
- **Rule induction:** only deterministic relations with at least two TRAIN receipts.
- **Validation:** at least two separate VALIDATE receipts; any contradiction forces HOLD for that key.
- **Authority:** exactly LOCAL_PREVIEW_ONLY, never external dispatch or permanent operator admission.
- **Typed quotient:** two worlds may share observable (decoder, topology) while differing in history digests.

## Frozen simulator and failure conditions

The evaluator fixes ten deterministic true transitions across two decoders *before training*; the learner does not import their table. It gets separately labeled synthetic TRAIN, VALIDATE, and TEST rows (two per key per split) plus a deliberately **false shortcut** with two misleading TRAIN confirmations and contradictory VALIDATE evidence.

The two decoders specifically disagree on FORK + curve:

    decoder A: FORK + curve -> CUP
    decoder B: FORK + curve -> LOOP

Proposed source traces use independently named records but are deterministic fixture duplicates; IDs do not demonstrate genuinely independent real witnesses.

**Prediction:** the typed validated table should cover exactly 20/22 held-out TEST records at 20/20 accuracy with **no** admission of the shortcut, and should complete two four-step routes starting at POINT. The frozen primitive grammar should stop after two operations. A context-blind table should be wrong on at least one of the two decoder-specific curve cases. Same-output histories must retain different digests. Unknown decoders and tampered receipts must HOLD.

**Null and stronger control:** a standard classical dictionary lookup given the **same decoded data** should match the learned version's outcomes. This is expected, not a defeat to hide. Improved performance relative to a deliberately weaker frozen grammar is no evidence of special generalization.

**Falsifiers:** shortcut admission, lost context contrast, loss of history, inconsistent predicted transitions, test-label leakage, unsafe provenance acceptance, unsound status escalation, or outperformance claims beyond the finite fixture.

**Scope warning:** the target labels, action sequences, decoder distinction and simulator are researcher-designed. The version here does not induce a visual topology from pixels, invent new operators beyond the fixed vocabulary, handle free human speech, run actual quantum circuits, infer missing physical laws, or update MOTIF production rules. The same 20/20 synthetic result can be produced by a conventional lookup table.
