# LEGIT-003 — THE GRAMMAR OF THE UNKNOWN
## A countermodel machine, not a consciousness detector

**Status:** experimental, draft, source- and authority-scoped.
**Question:** When a measured response is absent, have we established
the absence of the thing we were trying to measure?

LEGIT-002 established an important boundary: MOTIF's native finite Bayesian
reason router could not interpret a real scientific-evidence packet, and the
hand-authored neuroscience adapter could only check sample rates and refuse
unsupported posterior claims.

This experiment crosses one precise boundary: it **does not ask the engineer
to enumerate candidate worlds or invent priors**. Instead, it enumerates the
*admissible target assignments* implicit in the published observations. Two
fully specified, mutually contradictory completions are returned as an
inspectable certificate of non-identifiability.

It is an elementary finite partial-identification algorithm, with substantial
classical prior art. The experiment does NOT establish superior intelligence,
autonomous language understanding, or subjective experience.

### Mechanics

1. **Keep target separate from proxy:** the unseen `latent_target` and
   measurable `observed_proxy` must be distinct opaque identifiers.
2. **Preserve source provenance:** each observed cohort is tied to a declared
   source ID/URL; no source authentication or proof of cohort independence is
   claimed.
3. **Construct a complete finite model family:** each source observation
   has an integer number of proxy-positive and proxy-negative cases. With
   no validated latent-target labels, all target assignments from zero through
   the observed count are logically possible. Synthetic-only simulator
   labels can narrow those ranges.
4. **Build two concrete countermodels:** assign the minimum and maximum
   permitted latent-target counts in each observed cell, checking that both
   reproduce all admitted proxy counts and all declared synthetic constraints.
5. **Test the claim:** whether `proxy negative ⇒ target absent` holds in
   **every compatible completion of the admitted sample**. If it holds only
   in some, emit `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_FROM_ADMITTED_EVIDENCE`.
6. **Route the investigation:** find the negative-proxy cell with the
   largest unanchored range. Suggest an *unsent* research question about
   externally validating the measurement bridge. The heuristic is NOT
   calibrated information gain, and nothing is executed.

### The consciousness test (actual compiled source packet)

The previous LEGIT-002 research packet records two published NEJM cohort
groups: 241 behaviorally non-command-responsive patients, including 60 with
positive neural command-following; and 112 behaviorally command-responsive
patients, including 43 positive. The negative neural-test counts are 181 and
69, respectively.

From this **hand-curated aggregate packet**, our constructed compatible
target assignments include:

| Quantity in these 353 observations | Logical minimum | Logical maximum |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Unobserved subjective-experience labels among all cases | 0 | 353 |
| Unobserved subjective-experience labels among 250 negative-neural-test cases | 0 | 250 |

That broad interval is not a credible population estimate and does not assert
that the extremes actually occur. It is a constructive demonstration that
**the source counts alone do not identify the unobserved target**. The
extreme countermodels leave the real test observations unchanged. This result
depends on the admitted evidence model; it does not deny other evidence or
philosophical arguments about experience.

### Independent baseline and transfer stress

- **128** frozen, authored synthetic problems with randomized unknown-target
  assignments, heterogeneous proxy counts and simulator-certified partial
  labels.
- **Independent brute-force classical enumerator** literally enumerates
  every binary target assignment per tiny cell and checks label constraints.
- The compiler must match the exact classical min/max bounds and include the
  secret synthetic answer in its interval in every case.
- Test an intentionally opaque second domain (underwater sonar evidence about
  an unobserved property) by renaming target, proxy and group identifiers.
  Bounds must remain invariant: no `consciousness`-specific rule is involved.
- Fail closed on fabricated source IDs, schema changes, duplicate records,
  contradictory anchors, negative/float counts, unauthorized real-world
  target labels and attempted inference from no negative observations.

These synthetic problems are **not independently authored unseen real-world
challenges**. Tying the classical enumerator is the correct control outcome.
This is a small formal step in problem formulation, not an AI benchmark win.

### Crucial boundaries

**Source packet translation is handwritten.** `bridge.py` maps the prior
LEGIT-002 evidence records to this typed grammar; there is no autonomous
paper ingestion or raw-text-to-semantics step.

**The target is not measured.** Positive neural command following and
subjective consciousness are not silently made identical. A candidate
countermodel is a logical assignment of unknowns, not a diagnosed person,
an asserted human state, a Bayesian posterior, or a medical recommendation.

**Source IDs are not signatures.** The packet carries URLs and a digest, but
does not independently authenticate study methodology, ground-truth
consciousness, identity, patient counts, or source independence.

**Hypothesis space is generated, not learned from language.** The algorithm
enumerates compatible assignments in a declared finite grammar; it does not
discover a new mathematical law or arbitrary missing predicates.

**No automatic authority.** The router only suggests a research question,
never contacts people, orders clinical tests, executes tools, or modifies
external repositories. Clinical and human-study oversight remain external.

### Actual next architectural gate

A legitimate *Grammar of the Unknown II* would first ingest new textual
questions and independently authored, source-verifiable evidence; distinguish
an observed target from a proxy, detect hidden assumptions and common-source
echoes; then choose among countermodel construction, causal inference,
ordinary lookup, and explicit abstention. On **held-out subjects**, compare
this mechanism to a strong conventional semantic/parser plus symbolic solver.
Only transfer results on independent source questions would justify promotion.
