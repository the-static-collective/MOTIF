# MOTIF GENIUS-008 — STRANGE GENIUS ATLAS

**Research HOLD.** Interpretations of sources are experimental operator hypotheses, not endorsements, simulations of personalities, or quantitative rankings of human intelligence. Scriptural, apocalyptic, artistic, engineering and historical material have different evidence standards.

**Enoch × Bacon × Aquinas:** compare a multi-era witness graph with an objection/response ledger; prohibit promoting a later textual tradition into an earlier primary source.

**Povenmire × Acaster × Nolan × Shakespeare:** independent A/B/C narrative threads, delayed reinterpretive callback, nonlinear revelation, and distinct character knowledge.

**Jesus × Simmons × Oppenheimer:** a consent-first, accessible and accountable multi-stakeholder draft that values a neighbor's agency rather than treating humans as throughput.

The next verification stage should test each composition with independent task fixtures and strong conventional baselines, measuring source fidelity, contradiction retention, timing/plot integrity, participation constraints and actual downstream performance.
