## Independent authoring and source custody

Three independently authorized *public repository* branches were created as original implementation owners:

1. **ALEX.2 — `research/motif021-evidence-search`:** actually runs a source-locus exact-phrase search over five full pinned **public Markdown documents**, saves a 12-hit source-index and a research packet. Coverage is **PARTIAL** because the live web, changed branches, hidden documents, actual original witness carriers and private records were not scanned. No private data was exported.
2. **planZ — `research/motif021-classical-plan`:** actually computes a shortest-cost seven-action **STRIPS-like classical planning program** through finite Dijkstra search, cost 9. Stores the review-held proposed procedure. No planned step is executed merely because it is on the plan.
3. **GRAFT — `graft/motif021-compiled-capability`:** actually compiles a fixed allowlisted recipe and runs two executable JS capabilities: a source-locus review graph and a source-family nonindependence caution. Real source-owner approval, publication and deployment remain unperformed.

MOTIF-021 imports **16 byte-identical original donor code/data Git blobs** into `experiments/021/...` and Git-hash-verifies every copied blob before use. The donor sources are not rewritten, and all materializations remain scoped to the frozen public research packet. The independent source-owner branches remain the authority over their own changes.
