# RECIPROCAL-003 — Pre-registered two-door → reason field → method laboratory

**Frozen before implementing 003 code** (November 12, 2026). User approved crossing from purposeful inquiry through Reason Hypermap-style source history into a bounded Algorithm Foundry.

## Null / alternative hypotheses

- H0: Given source-scoped answers from volunteers, a finite local reason field adds **no objectively verified human or machine capabilities**; conditional-rule selection does not beat a strong classical conditional learner and may overfit.
- H1 (limited): When explicitly reported answers in an authored fixture vary by self-declared experience role, a **role-conditioned method** trained on earlier source reports may predict later *reports* better than a global-plurality method. It may also do worse under adversarial/shift cases. This is classical pattern learning, not novel mathematical discovery or truth of the original question.
- H2 (behavioral): The local system can preserve ambiguity/contests, preview two hypothetical outcomes without asserting them as evidence, and refuse to promote a learned candidate or solicit anyone without opt-in. This is an engineering claim verifiable by tests.

## Mechanism, agreed before seeing results

1. Take the EXACT existing RECIPROCAL-002 state schema and `TwoDoors.effective/inspect` from the parent source. Do not replace the owner or fabricate people.
2. Project the evidence into a new **distinct** versioned source-scoped inquiry graph (not into the original `motif.reason-hypermap/v0` schema, which is restricted to fictional eight-hypothesis owner probes). Use explicit question, proposed option, source-report, decision, role, and missing-distinction nodes. Represent one-to-many/many-to-many associations as typed, multi-terminal hyperedges and keep recorded reports separate from hypothetical counterfactuals.
3. Only for an eligible open question, produce `what if one additional person reported answer k?` branches using a cloned TwoDoors state with an explicitly fictional source root, enumerate each of its 2–5 answer options. Do **not** count these imagined answers in the actual ledger. Report what changes about inquiry **status**, not what must be true.
4. A method candidate may only be induced from pairs of distinct self-declared role groups with **at least three source roots per role**. Training uses the **first two chronological observations per role**, and testing uses the **remaining at least one per role**, never used in fitting. Role-conditioned prediction is simple per-role plurality with ties resolved by lowest option index. A matched global-majority baseline learns from exactly the same training observations. Both are classical algorithms. The role rule is proposed **only if** its number of correct *later reported option values* exceeds global-majority by at least **two**; it still needs independent human review and cannot silently change app question routing or answer publication.
5. Hold and show reasons for insufficient samples, source contests, missing group contrast, tied benefit, or worse transfer. Do not infer human source identity from pseudonyms.
6. The display should make three things inspectable: (a) evidence versus hypothetical, (b) whether to ask/stop/reframe, and (c) candidate method fit on earlier versus later reported responses. No live network, profile, private project data or background monitoring.

## Measurement / falsifiers

- Tests for real parent integration (an actual RECIPROCAL-002 question/event state), source-collision handling, heldout isolation, reversed opinions, missing role classes, independence caveats, app script syntax and offline bundle correctness.
- Construct **one** author-designed synthetic role-dependent case, **one** uniform-answer case, **one** adversarial role-shift case, **one** duplicate-source-contest case, **one** insufficient-sources case, and **one** expiry/budget case. Use deterministic source-observation order and compare role versus global predictions on a heldout slice.
- Score correct predictions of **independently entered reported choices**, not verified factual truth, real human gains, consent authenticity, future usefulness, or causal effectiveness.
- Stop conditions: do not admit candidate models from duplicate roots or from contested source ledgers. Do not announce superior novel algorithm performance over strong classical conditional baselines, which can reproduce the result exactly.
- Strongest not-yet-tested question: does a learned strategy improve independent human decision outcomes *after action* relative to cost-aware classical active learning? No such data exists in this prototype.
