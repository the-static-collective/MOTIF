## Contemplative council — real mechanism, unproven utility

The final diagnostic implementation constructs **five programmed heuristic perspective cards** (skeptic, systems, story, planner, experimenter) for each eligible analogy. Across 128 eligible cases, MOTIF created **640 PENCIL_UNTESTED question cards**; the conventional single-critic portfolio created **640 question cards with the same evidence budget**. These cards did not change the intervention choices or access the owner verdict. They were NOT separately evaluated for real-world usefulness; 5 heuristic templates are not five autonomous agents, not conversations among independent geniuses, and not measured novel math.

Each cross-domain candidate was also projected into a **real native Free Graph** packet (`fg_core` plus `fg_ops.neighbors`), preserving only unresolved, nonconstituting links and a task-world cut marked unresolved. Structural analogical matching itself is implemented in separate conventional Python exhaustive graph-edit search, not natively by Free Graph. MOTIF-003 genuinely generated possible TEST affordances; it did not implement the underlying graph-isomorphism search or a new learned retrieval mechanism.

Private MEMENTO native Understory validation succeeded through the read-only owner bridge in private CI. Public CI with no private checkout held explicitly rather than impersonating verification. GHoT's actual pinned decision function gated synthetic, foreground-idle preparation conditions; no actual phone or cloud worker executed background cognition.

### Negative/uncertainty findings

- The **false-friend** regime has the same observable omission as a genuinely partial relation, but the true intervention differs. Both source-aware approximate matchers had to use a target diagnostic TEST to discriminate; their candidate was wrong in all 32 such cases, and correctly resulted in zero repairs. **Structural similarity is not causal proof**.
- The **echo-source** regime uses two separate receipt IDs with one underlying synthetic origin, and every provenance-aware informed controller refused them as independent corroboration.
- In the **unfamiliar** regime, no old source matched; refusing to pretend a fix was known was appropriate under the given two-test rule.
- All prior source-world interventions and synthetic witness-root labels were **programmer supplied**, not collected from genuinely independent people, institutions or external experiments. Different strings are not proof of independent human witnesses.
- The 768 cases are permutations of **five author-designed typed mechanism templates**. The common ontology and candidate fixes are already present in task data. This demonstrates cross-domain structural mapping, but **not genuinely novel causal laws or open-vocabulary discovery**.
- Modeled graph comparisons are an instrumented operation count, not CPU/energy savings. The extra five-lens question portfolio may cost additional resources; wall time and true compute parity were not benchmarked.

### Corrections and provenance
Initial CI caught one overly strict test assertion: the exact-isomorphism baseline had correctly **abstained** from a false friend rather than probing. This was repaired in the test without changing the underlying model's intervention policy. A later *post-hoc non-decisional diagnostic extension* generated the matched five-card heuristic councils and a new unit test; outcomes were rerun on the same previously examined cohort, so the last run is a regression confirmation of the intervention scores, NOT a newly blinded scientific replication. The full 18-test, 768-case final rerun passed, and the private owner integration pinned that final tested commit.

## Next genuine frontier
Bring an independently authored, license-compatible cross-domain analogy/problem corpus, keep target decisions held out, require a real source owner for consequential actions, and compare with mature structure-mapping, graph-matching, graph-edit, embedding and hybrid retrieval methods on equal *measured* computation. Permit analogies to mislead and the conventional baseline to win. The scientific question is whether learned schemas can discover new transferable relations under raw-language ambiguity instead of receiving their relational ontology as programmer labels.
