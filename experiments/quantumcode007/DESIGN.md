# QC-007 — THE LIVING MEANING FIELD / CONTINUING ACTION NETWORK

**Status:** Classical, deterministic, synthetic research-only experiment. Proposed branch stacked on open QC-006. No real food provider queried, no emergency resources verified, no user account accessed, no actions dispatched, no automatic model installation. No performance advantage over an established general planner has been demonstrated.

## The correction

User intent: **a symbol is a persistent carrier; it accrues meaning over time. The present field of action makes a *field of meanings* relevant. Meaning need not collapse into one correct answer.** Solutions to human problems may arise along very different paths, and today's achievement is not the final node of a person's life.

This is **not** QC-006's previous “choose the single correct decoder” architecture. QC-006 was a bounded demonstration of multiple independently validated mathematical readings; QC-007 is a **design correction** and a new executable **action-relative projection**.

## Mathematical scaffold: separate memory, relevance, action and consequence

```text
CARRIER / SYMBOL  -- append-only witnessed associations --> M_t
              \\                                    /
               \\-- context of available actions --/
                                 |
                         relevance aperture
                                 v
               F_t = R(M_t, A_t, needs_t)
                        {m1,m2,m3,...}
                                 |
                          possible actions
                   {path_a, path_b, path_c, ...}
                                 |
                  preview and multiobjective pressure
                                 v
              bounded non-dominated action *fiber*
                                 |
            consent + independent real-world validation
                                 |
                      real action / receipt
                                 |
                      newly claimed/witnessed meaning
                                 |
                   M_(t+1) = append(M_t, witnessed delta)
```

**The last step is NOT automatic in QC-007.** The code can **receive** a source-attributed outcome report and retain an unverified hypothesis about it. It neither executes real actions nor upgrades self-reports to independently verified reality. An unexpected outcome causes `HOLD_MODEL_REVIEW`, not an autonomous rewrite of the environment.

### Four distinguishable graph/semantic objects

1. **Meaning history:** immutable carrier-linked records with typed meaning kind, contextual affordances, original source ID and order of accrual. Accumulation is append-only. A later meaning cannot overwrite the earlier historical snapshot. Typed links include `SUPPORTS`, `TENSIONS`, `CO_OCCURS`, `REINTERPRETS`, `POSSIBLE_ROUTE`, etc.
2. **Current action field:** time horizon, current needs, available resources, consent/feasibility assumptions and permitted risk. This is the conditioning context, **not a universal judge of true meaning**.
3. **Relevance aperture:** selects all directly relevant meanings and one-hop related meanings; keeps contradictions typed as tensions. A field view is an ephemeral projection with an immutable source receipt. It doesn't delete unselected historical meanings.
4. **Action network:** transitions from a state to candidate next states, with typed preconditions, risk, duration, spend and modeled effects. A “solution” is a node with possible outgoing transitions and further needs, not a terminal award.

A fifth object, **`SemanticActionHypothesis`**, arises when two relevant meanings have a typed relation suggesting a possible new bridge between affordances. This is returned as **`HOLD_UNVERIFIED_ACTION_AFFORDANCE`** with a concrete research question, not granted a made-up resource effect or permission to dispatch an unverified action.

## Deliberately heterogeneous solution forms: nourishment

The exemplar deliberately distinguishes **immediate hunger** from **recurring food insecurity**:

- **Acute:** at least one *modeled meal unit* within **60 minutes** of hypothetical activity. Both a hypothetical nearby community meal and contacting someone for food, then using that food, can qualify; neither availability nor arrival was checked.
- **Persistent:** at least one modeled meal unit **and** at least **seven modeled days of continued access** within a **180-minute action-planning budget**. The latter is a *researcher-entered prediction*, not a guaranteed week of food. An individual meal alone does **not** satisfy the longer-term criterion.
- **Isolated:** no phone, transport or stored food; the bounded action inventory finds no safe path and must HOLD, not invent availability or claim fasting solves hunger.
- **Stored supplies:** current usable food and a kitchen are available; direct nourishment may be preferable to elaborate coordination.

Model has explicit gates: unverified foraging, illegal activity and replacing nutrition with fasting are never eligible as food solutions. A brief breathing/focus practice may increase a modeled non-nutritive stress tool, but cannot create a food unit. This is a safety distinction, not medical advice.

All timings, food units, acceptance at communal organizations, cash costs, and hypothetical outcomes are **fabricated scenario constants** for engineering tests, not sourced real-world service availability. If this were connected to real care work, service eligibility, actual availability, safe access, transportation, consent, dietary requirements and local circumstances must be established, and unmet urgent need would require human handling.

## Bounded search / no singular optimization score

The planner enumerates source-pinned counterfactual states up to **three actions** and **4,000 states**, with safety, time and budget constraints. It returns every **Pareto-nondominated** path, including tied paths with different action histories. It does not use a secret scalar reward to force one best answer or quietly select one meaning to be the final meaning.

For the toy nourishment state, goals are modeled as multi-dimensional food units, days of access, coordination links, supplemental stress tools, and costs in time/money/risk. These are **researcher choices**; they are not a universal moral utility scale. `continuations` reports the next feasible actions at each non-dominated node.

A deliberately weak **one-step baseline** picks the available action that maximizes immediate modeled meal units. It satisfies the acute goal but cannot satisfy the recurring-access goal. That is an illustration of *planning horizon*, **not** a claim of advantage over classical AI planning (which already supports multi-stage, multi-objective reasoning).

## Real-world structural transport: same kernel, different domains

A separate, **domain-neutral resource/goal kernel** implements exactly the same kind of graph search using arbitrary nonnegative resource coordinates, required thresholds, objective dimensions and typed hypothetical effects. The optimizer itself does not contain `if domain == event` or `if domain == electronics` logic. Two fixture adapters declare:

1. **Community gathering:** site confirmed, recruiting volunteers, preparing or sharing food, issuing invitations, and optional follow-up relationships. Two alternate ways to prepare an event’s food and continued contact can be present in the same non-dominated fiber.
2. **Equipment function:** diagnosis, route tests, acquiring hypothetical compatible parts, repairing the route or borrowing a fallback, followed optionally by documenting the connection. Restoration and continuation need not require the same operation.

This is *code reuse and representation portability*, **not** empirical proof that the device can be repaired, volunteers will show up, or event food can be acquired.

Additional source-attributed negative controls include historical snapshot replay, duplicate IDs, backdated associations, false ancestry/reinterpretation edges, stale action apertures, unsafe substitutes, resource budget exhaustion, duplicate actions, invalid effect types, counterfactual ≠ externally verified, and unexpected outcomes that preserve history and request review.

## Research integrity / real-world limits

- A local source digest is not an identity signature, independent external witness, lawful permission or proof that a provider accepted someone.
- No connected external services, schedules, mail delivery, eligibility tools, financial transactions, live food availability, or human contacts were used.
- The search space is finite and researcher-declared: it is **not** literally the infinity of edible foods or all possible human actions.
- This planner does not solve hunger, poverty, organizational coordination or hardware restoration. Those require resources, trustworthy observations, cooperation, lawful permissions, physical access and follow-through. Optimization alone cannot supply the missing world.
- New action ideas from symbolic relations are **hypotheses to test**; their causality, safety and feasibility must be established before introducing quantitative predicted effects.
- There is no independently authored blind benchmark and no matched computational baseline against standard multiobjective planners. The one-step baseline is intentionally weak.
- No quantum algorithm, unrestricted mathematical invention, autonomous algorithm creation or recursive self-improvement has been demonstrated.
