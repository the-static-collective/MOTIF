## The created source-owned instrument

Unlike 005's completed sensor menu, 006 is given only six source-owned edge-read atoms and generic bounded AND/OR operations. It synthesizes a proposed **read-only measurement program** from 1,561 candidate monotone-DNF expressions. For the source-owned synthetic world in which any of three two-hop routes can connect the endpoints, it inferred exactly:

```text
(READ_LINK(0) AND READ_LINK(1))
    OR
(READ_LINK(2) AND READ_LINK(3))
    OR
(READ_LINK(4) AND READ_LINK(5))
```

The resulting `motif.law-forge.instrument-program.v1` packet is a strict data-only executable local sensor AST, carrying an evidence digest and `PROPOSE_ONLY` status.

The learner did **not** receive a ready-made reachability sensor, but the owner disclosed all six link identities, the graph topology and the allowed AND/OR grammar. It did **not** invent a new programming-language primitive, unknown physical instrument, or unbounded method. The research uses a complete 64-configuration finite factorial panel after 32 owner-approved additional local queries, not optimized active inquiry.

The derived sensor passed **seven of the thirteen authored mechanism families**. The complete six-link raw lookup was a **stronger predictor** than the invented compact expression. The invented sensors consulted an average of **3.0 distinct raw source links** in the seven eligible synthetic families under the declared program structure. This is a static AST complexity count. The 64-case training observation panel read all six links, so **no actual acquisition, computation, network, energy or hardware efficiency saving was demonstrated**.

## Scientific limitations

The owner supplied six link addresses, graph topology, a binary outcome interface and AND/OR. The finite graph worlds and all mechanisms were written by the experiment designer. Seeds change only synthetic participant identity, not hidden mechanism laws. SHA-256 fingerprints identify bytes but not true witnesses or authority. The experiment did not learn where unknown link sensors exist or safely create a new source adapter from an unfamiliar API; **real-world instrument invention remains unproven**.

The presence of three relays, three source-owned Dogram specimens and three permitted DNF terms carries **no trinary metaphysical privilege**. It is simply this experiment's chosen finite benchmark.
