# MOTIF GEOM-006 — The Staircase Lab / Temporal Relational Atlas

**Status:** executable **synthetic** research, isolated branch, no external integration or real-world action. Built as an independent child of the open GEOM-005 experiment, not `main`. Python standard library only.

## Why this exists

The August 27 Daily Slice proposed **Flower = latent nodemap**, **Megatron/Metatron's Cube = edgemap**, **solids = stable larger forms**, and a **moving temporal braid**. Its crucial corrections were (1) two old nodes may gain a newly *perceived* edge, (2) that does not make their causal history new, and (3) resemblance does not prove ancestry. National Treasure's **Same State, Different World**, **The Peach / Coherence Space** and **Primal Overlap** supply the identifiability, configuration-space and generative-topology controls. The 3rdi owner distinguishes occurrence, availability, attention and relevance.

This experiment tests the **mathematical distinctions**, not sacred geometry, hidden codes, metaphysical time, novelty of classical algorithms or historical provenance of the diagram. Coordinates are explicitly a removable display-only option. The 13 positions are a convenient bounded fixture, **not a law about 13 or intelligence**.

## Four clocks and two kinds of graph

- `Place.occurred_at`: when the node occurred in synthetic world history.
- `Relation.effective_from/effective_until`: when a relational claim purports to apply in world history. `effective_until` is exclusive.
- `Relation.discovered_at`: when the claimed relation was noticed/recorded.
- `Relation.admitted_at/withdrawn_at`: when a **fixture author** declared a bounded working admission/withdrawal. These are **not validated THOUGHT-006 pen receipts** and do not establish real evidence or authority.
- `visible_to[observer]`: explicit first availability to the named observer. Unlisted observers see nothing. Evidence visibility may be later than the relation's recorded discovery.
- `world_cut` and `knowledge_cut`: independently requested coordinates. Reconstruct **world at W, as knowable to observer O at K**. A cut is a query, not a change to historical nodes.

The `view()` result retains visible pencil claims and withdrawn admissions with their status and source label, without converting disagreement into historical fact. `earliest_route()` uses only explicit, direction-respecting, declared-admitted `route` relations. It is a **time-respecting earliest-arrival path**: waiting is permitted, traversal consumes positive time, and a hop must finish before a route's closing time. A direct resemblance never makes a route. An aggregate static graph can falsely claim reachability that the temporal graph refuses.

A separate `select_question()` routine holds multiple proposed worlds, uses known categorical predictions and declared synthetic priors, and chooses a **permitted synthetic observation proposal** by classical Shannon information gain divided by cost. It does not identify a winner, execute a probe, assume missing predictions, or convert a high-information but prohibited probe into permission.

## Adversarial controls

1. **Hindsight leak:** no late discovery in a past knowledge cut; viewing old world-history with later knowledge must not rewrite the earlier cut.
2. **Observer parallax:** two observers can view identical world history but legitimately receive different known relation sets.
3. **No visual promotion:** a pretty geometric connection remains pencil, cannot create identity, support, admission or traversal.
4. **Temporal false positive:** two individually admitted edges can be connected in an aggregate diagram but impossible in chronological order.
5. **Retraction:** a withdrawn record stays in the historical view but ceases to be traversable after withdrawal.
6. **Directionality:** no reversing a directed edge merely because a line looks symmetric.
7. **Identifiability:** two worlds producing the same observations stay indistinguishable.
8. **Capability fence:** only fixture-permitted probes can be *proposed*, never executed. No external authority in any output.
9. **Classical controls:** earliest arrival uses standard Dijkstra with waiting, not novel geometric intelligence; question choice uses classical entropy partitioning. Compare against independent time-expanded dynamic programming and brute categorical partitions when extending the benchmark.
10. **Metaphor removal:** NONE view returns the same semantic claims as HEX view.
