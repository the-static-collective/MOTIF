03 / Not a popularity contest

Reasoning field

The engine compares claimed independent sources, watches for disagreement, respects asking limits, and proposes when to review or reframe. A leading response is not automatically true.

04 / Human-controlled evidence

Keep the trace. Own the trace.

Responses live in this browser session by default. You can explicitly save locally, export all source-scoped events, or import voluntary contributions from other devices. No data is automatically sent to MOTIF, GitHub, or another person.

Nothing is automatically shared.

Importing data never means authentication: pseudonyms, roles, and evidence claims are unverified. Same-source conflicts are retained rather than silently counted as independent confirmations. Do not collect sensitive personal details in this prototype.
