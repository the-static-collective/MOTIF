## The critical connection to MOTIF-018

The prior MOTIF-018 result expands a **fixed** two-hop graph-feature search and obtains 100% accuracy on a tiny, invented, perfectly measured family. A conventional exhaustive feature-search method ties. The proposal is held.

The Green Grammar readings demand stronger distinctions:

\`\`\`text
signal / retained carrier
      ↓ observer-local aperture + unresolved gap
candidate sense field, not forced meaning
      ↓ hypothesis/relation generation
IDEA / FACT_CANDIDATE / RELATION_CANDIDATE
      ↓ declared transform, exact source, typed projection
representation with its operator and an attributable delta
      ↓ independent evidence, corrections, other observers
discriminator, alternative hypotheses, admitted or HOLD
      ↓ consequential third position
source receipt → HOLD → destination consent/admission
      ↓
new capability only if an independent world shows new feasible
AND valuable AND permitted actions.
\`\`\`

This grammar matters because **the model's new vocabulary and the world's newly accessible information are not the same thing**. A representation search over raw edges already supplied is not invention of raw sensory data.

## The failure worth prioritizing

**FALSIFIER A — GRAPH SHADOW != THE WORLD.**

With the *identical raw graph* but an independently changed real outcome, the held 018 predictor can be **confidently wrong on every case**. Its accuracy is 0.00 on this hostile synthetic batch.

This is not a failed unit test; it is an intentionally preserved **counterexample to a stronger scientific claim**. No internal checksum or source-name string can authenticate the truth of a physical observation. MOTIF needs an independent source/effect/observer model before it can responsibly turn that inference into a world claim.

**FALSIFIER B — GRAMMAR INVENTION IS NOT YET OPEN-ENDED.**

A three-edge path or an XOR of two added relations cannot be expressed by the current hard-coded one- and two-edge candidate grammar, even when all the necessary edges are visible. MOTIF properly returns HOLD rather than inventing a feature it did not actually construct. To exceed this, 019 needs bounded typed-grammar expansion, growth costs, independent holdouts, and conventional synthesis baselines.

**FALSIFIER C — LOSSLESS RAW RETENTION MATTERS.**

When the low-level graph edges are *removed*, not merely hidden from the current feature table, no invented representation can reconstruct which world produced a given repeated x. This is an information-theoretic obstruction, not an optimization bug. An unknown must sometimes prompt **new measurement**, not a new story.

## Important untested aspects of the long Green Grammar road

The following are *not* validated by this fixture:

- Actual two-stranger negotiated meaning over time; human intention; durable care, attention and consent.
- Natural language ambiguity in the open world, or speaker-specific semantic drift.
- True independent witness authentication, out-of-band custody, forgery detection and live chronology verification.
- Live Dogram, ALEX.2, 3rdi, Iron Lung, National Treasure or JAR;VIS bindings.
- Unbounded growth of a concept grammar or automatic revision of the diagnostic's objective.
- Conservation, spiritual, metaphysical, or theological claims.
- Genuine affordance gain in a physically observed environment.

## MOTIF-019 / Green Grammar next design, contingent on these findings

1. **Representation is not observation.** Give MOTIF a budgeted choice between \`NEW_CONCEPT\`, \`NEW_MEASUREMENT\`, \`NEW_DISCRIMINATOR\` and \`HOLD\`. Preserve observer cuts, raw receipts and acquisition costs.
2. **Make grammar itself revisable, but bounded.** Allow typed operator-composition depth to expand when observed contradictions justify it. Use a description-length or compute penalty and independent controls to resist vacuous grammar inflation.
3. **Keep explanations plural.** Track a finite hypothesis set with unresolved incompatible interpretations; don't turn top-ranked guesses into ground truth. Connect a reviewed 014W witness model and 015/016 multi-road inquiry only through source-owned contracts.
4. **Score nontrivial new abilities.** On sealed graph domains, track new correct *permitted* tasks, new question types and net regret against strong Bayesian/program-synthesis methods; penalize counterfactual inventions that create no benefit.
5. **Require a third position for consequential crossing.** Source-hand and destination-hand, reviewed authority, lineage and a returnable HOLD receipt. Learning a method does not authorize using it on external systems.

**The central research claim is not that "all is grammar." It is that a faithful system should know when its grammar is not enough, preserve what it doesn't know, and seek the particular observation or transformation needed to move honestly.**
